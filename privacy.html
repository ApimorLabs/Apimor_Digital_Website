Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

At Apimor Digital, accessible from apimordigital.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and clients. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by Apimor Digital and how we use it across our website, digital platforms (such as Invitely), and hardware systems (such as HiveTrace and Automated Water Vending Machines).

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information directly from you, automatically through your interactions with our platforms, and via telemetry from our deployed hardware devices:

  • Personal Identifiable Information: Name, email address, phone number, and physical billing/shipping location when you contact us, request a quote, or register for services like Invitely.
  • Device and Telemetry Data: For systems like HiveTrace and our Water Vending Machines, we collect operational variables (e.g., environmental data, load weights, dispensation volumes, error logs, and system uptime) necessary to maintain service performance.
  • Log Files & Analytics: Standard connection data via Cloudflare, including internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, and pages visited.

2. How We Use Your Information

Apimor Digital processes data under legitimate commercial and analytical grounds, specifically to:

  • Provide, operate, troubleshoot, and maintain our hardware and software ecosystems.
  • Process transactions for our digital products and agro/machinery wholesale inventory.
  • Send critical technical notifications, system updates, and respond directly to customer service inquiries.
  • Analyze macro-usage trends to optimize system hardware performance and app interfaces.

3. Data Storage, Hosting, and Security

Our web operations are securely powered by Cloudflare Pages. We enforce robust technical and organizational security measures to shield your personal records from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, please note that no electronic transmission or storage framework can be guaranteed as 100% secure.

6. Your Data Rights & Deletion (Erasure) Requests

Depending on your location, you hold comprehensive legal rights regarding your personal information under the Tanzanian Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and global privacy standards. Most notably, you have the right to access your data, correct inaccuracies, or request the **complete erasure** of your personal records from our systems.

How to Request Data Deletion:

If you have an account on our platforms (such as Invitely) or have shared information with us, you can initiate a permanent data deletion request at any time using one of the following methods:

  • By Email: Send an email to contact@apimordigital.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request. Please send the request from the email address associated with the account you want deleted.
  • By Phone / WhatsApp: Reach out directly to our operations team at +255 743 527 350 to verify your identity and request data removal.

*Note: Once verified, we will securely purge your data from our active user databases within 30 days. We only retain minimal data where required by law (such as transaction history for corporate tax reporting).

5. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent out your personal identification metrics to third parties. We may employ trusted external cloud vendors (like email routing or database engines) strictly to execute operational tasks, subject to stringent confidentiality mandates.

6. Your Data Rights

Depending on your location, you hold legal rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access to the data we store, request corrections to erroneous entries, or demand complete erasure of your record from our systems under allowed circumstances.
